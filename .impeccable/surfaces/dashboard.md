# Dashboard surface

Mode: Operate

FORM seed key: `digital-design-canon-dark-first-developer-console`

This selected FORM was corroborated against the built surface: a persistent dark console, fine structural seams, narrow operational color, compact labelled telemetry, and an action path that remains visible without turning the fleet into a card grid.

## Direction contract

The dashboard is a dark-first developer control surface built like a persistent switching console: graphite planes meet at hairline seams; live state alone receives color; the runner relay reads as one continuous backplane rather than a pile of cards.

### First viewport

The fleet is already visible. A narrow signal rail carries the aggregate availability state into full-width runner channels. Each channel answers, in one scan: whether traffic can reach it, which alias clients use, what vLLM reports, how much context is known, how loaded it is, and whether LiteLLM synchronized it.

### Signature interaction

Every successful refresh sends one restrained pulse down the fleet backplane. Runner state changes do not animate in from nowhere; their status lamp and plain-language availability copy change in place.

### State vocabulary

- green: connected and routable through the relay;
- amber: enrolled/connecting or gateway synchronization needs attention;
- red: revoked or failed;
- neutral: offline, with last-seen time preserved;
- destructive controls are isolated from ordinary actions and require confirmation.

### Responsive behavior

At wide widths, the fleet and enrollment runbook share one divided work surface. At narrow widths they become a single ordered column; runner measurements wrap into labelled pairs without horizontal scrolling.

### Honest risk

Developer consoles can become indistinguishable fields of dark rectangles. This surface avoids that by using one continuous backplane, clear typographic hierarchy, full-width channels, and color only for operational state.
